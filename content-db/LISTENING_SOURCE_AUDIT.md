## Scope and limits

- Rechecked all 86 records including the 53 previously lacking Cambridge labels.
- Fetched 104 complete reference tests: Cambridge IELTS 1–21, Official Guide 1–8 and both Trainer volumes.
- Compared each of the four app sections separately using six-word sequences from question stems and contextual stimulus; required the strongest match to the same complete test for every section.
- Compared further against accessible Practice Tests Plus references; classified source failures as unavailable evidence, not negative matches.
- Cambridge identities here have secondary-source content corroboration; book families are supported by publisher descriptions.
- This audits provenance, not all answer keys or audio timing.
- The 35 unresolved sets are not labelled unofficial or fabricated.
- Trainer is Cambridge practice material and is counted separately from official examination books.
- Commercial and unresolved sets are preserved for historical review; current selection policy keeps the 45 identifiable Cambridge sets.

All original data and completed attempts are retained. The JSON contains per-section evidence counts and original imported labels.

## Runtime policy

`listeningIdentities.json` contains the 45 source identities and source fingerprints. `listeningAvailability.js` applies the reversible selection policy to catalogs and adapters. All 86 bank records are preserved. Review-only lookups retain old questions and answer keys; excluded unfinished sessions cannot resume. Selection tests cover all aliases and both mock assemblers.
